pbr2rad

PBR to Radiance Material Converter Experimental

About pbr2rad

What it does

  • Converts a PBR texture set (ambientCG · Poly Haven · your uploads) into a ready-to-use Radiance material.
  • The zip: images as HDR/.dat, the .cal projection, a plastic/metal primitive with calibrated reflectance, and a preview.

Why

  • Textured Radiance materials are normally expert-mode: hand-written .cal, HDR conversion, guessed reflectance.
  • pbr2rad does the conversion and reports the visible reflectance, so results stay defensible for lighting analysis.

Intended workflow · ClimateStudio

  • Pick or upload materials → tune against the reference → Download → drop the folder into ClimateStudio's custom materials library → assign in Rhino.
  • The output is plain Radiance; it works in any Radiance-based tool.

Status

  • An experiment by Jeff Thompson (not a Solemma product), built with approval from the ClimateStudio developers, who have accommodated pbr2rad materials on their side.
  • Textures are CC0 (ambientCG, Poly Haven).
  • Questions or ideas: jt@thompsonjeff.com.

Known limitations

  • ClimateStudio support: pbr2rad materials will likely only appear correctly in ClimateStudio material browser from the latest v2.4x stable release candidate onward; earlier builds may not display textured materials properly in the material browser.
  • Some ambientCG wood references are warmer and brighter than their own colour maps; our sphere shows the map as it is, which is what ClimateStudio will render.
  • Textures are capped at 2k for conversion.
  • Perforated / cut-out materials (anything with an opacity map) convert as solid surfaces for now.
  • Displacement and ambient-occlusion maps are not used; colour, roughness, metalness and normal maps drive the material.
  • The preview's studio HDRI isn't the source sites' studio, so mirror-metal reflections won't match theirs exactly.
